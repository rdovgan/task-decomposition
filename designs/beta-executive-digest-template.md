## Tone & Style Guidelines

**Do:**
✅ Lead with the bottom line
✅ Be concise (executive should read in <3 minutes)
✅ Use red/yellow/green for quick scanning
✅ Include business impact, not just feature details
✅ Be transparent about issues
✅ Ask for decisions clearly
✅ Provide data to back up claims

**Don't:**
❌ Bury the lead
❌ Use technical jargon
❌ Make it too long
❌ Hide bad news
❌ Be vague about timelines
❌ Sugarcoat risks

## Week-Specific Executive Themes

### Week 1: Launch & Initial Signals
**Focus:** Early engagement, critical bugs, onboarding success
**Key question:** Is beta working? Are testers engaged?

### Week 2: Pattern Recognition
**Focus:** Repeat usage, identifying trends, common workflows
**Key question:** What's working vs. what's not?

### Week 3: Feature Validation
**Focus:** Which features land, which miss, user patterns
**Key question:** Are we solving the right problems?

### Week 4: Mid-Beta Decision Point
**Focus:** Go/no-go assessment, pivot decisions, timeline
**Key question:** Should we continue, pivot, or stop?

### Final Week: Launch Readiness
**Focus:** Final assessment, launch recommendation, transition
**Key question:** Are we ready to launch?

## Pre-Send Checklist

Before sending the executive digest:

- [ ] All metrics are accurate and current
- [ ] Red/yellow/green scores are justified
- [ ] Blockers are clearly flagged
- [ ] Decisions needed are specific and actionable
- [ ] Tone is concise and executive-appropriate
- [ ] Business impact is clear (not just features)
- [ ] Recommendations are backed by data
- [ ] Timeline estimates are realistic
- [ ] Escalations are justified and specific
- [ ] CEO's preferred format is followed
- [ ] Sensitive info is anonymized
