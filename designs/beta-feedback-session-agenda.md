# Weekly Beta Feedback Session Agenda

**Duration:** 60 minutes
**Frequency:** Every Friday, 3-4 PM PT
**Location:** Zoom / Google Meet
**Facilitator:** Product Manager (PM)
**Participants:** Beta testers, Product team, Engineering lead

---

## Agenda Template

### 0. Welcome & Logistics (2 minutes)
- ⏰ 3:00-3:02 PM
- 🎯 **Goal:** Review week's feedback and prioritize improvements
- 📋 **Housekeeping:**
  - Recording shared after session
  - Chat open for real-time feedback
  - Be specific and constructive

---

### 1. Wins & Celebrations (8 minutes)
- ⏰ 3:02-3:10 PM
- 🎯 **Goal:** Share what's working well

**Discussion Questions:**
- What was your biggest win this week?
- What feature made your life easier?
- Any pleasant surprises or "aha!" moments?

**Capture Format:**
- [ ] Feature/Workflow that worked
- [ ] Who mentioned it
- [ ] Why it was valuable
- [ ] Action: Document for case study / testimonials

---

### 2. Pain Points & Challenges (15 minutes)
- ⏰ 3:10-3:25 PM
- 🎯 **Goal:** Identify top 3 blockers to fix this week

**Discussion Questions:**
- What frustrated you most this week?
- Where did you feel stuck or confused?
- What workflow felt inefficient?

**Prioritization Framework:**
- 🔴 **Critical:** Blocks core workflow, affects >50% of users
- 🟡 **High:** Frequent pain point, affects 20-50% of users
- 🟢 **Medium:** Annoying but workaround exists

**Output:** Top 3 prioritized issues with owners

---

### 3. Feature Requests (12 minutes)
- ⏰ 3:25-3:37 PM
- 🎯 **Goal:** Collect and validate feature ideas

**Discussion Questions:**
- What feature would help you most?
- What's missing from your workflow?
- Any "wouldn't it be cool if..." ideas?

**Capture Format:**
- [ ] Feature description
- [ ] Use case / problem it solves
- [ ] Proposed user benefit
- [ ] Effort estimate (Quick win vs. Major feature)

**Quick Vote:**
- Use emoji reactions to prioritize top requests
- 👍 = "Need this"
- 🤔 = "Nice to have"
- 👎 = "Not for me"

---

### 4. Bug Triage (15 minutes)
- ⏰ 3:37-3:52 PM
- 🎯 **Goal:** Review reported bugs and assign owners

**Process:**
1. Review bugs from Slack #task-tool-beta
2. Categorize by severity:
   - 🔥 **P0 - Critical:** Data loss, security, blocks all users
   - 🔴 **P1 - High:** Major feature broken, affects >50% users
   - 🟡 **P2 - Medium:** Feature degraded, affects <50% users
   - 🟢 **P3 - Low:** Cosmetic, minor inconvenience
3. Assign owner and target fix date
4. Confirm workaround (if applicable)

**Bug Report Template for Session:**
```
**Bug Title:** [One-line summary]
**Severity:** [P0/P1/P2/P3]
**Affected Users:** [# or %]
**Steps to Reproduce:** [1, 2, 3...]
**Expected:** [What should happen]
**Actual:** [What actually happens]
**Workaround:** [Is there one?]
**Owner:** [@person]
**Target Fix:** [Date]
```

---

### 5. Action Items & Next Steps (5 minutes)
- ⏰ 3:52-3:57 PM
- 🎯 **Goal:** Confirm owners and deadlines

**Template:**
| Issue | Owner | Due Date | Status |
|-------|-------|----------|--------|
| [Pain point #1] | [@person] | [Date] | 🔴 In Progress |
| [Feature #1] | [@person] | [Date] | 🟡 Backlog |
| [Bug #1] | [@person] | [Date] | 🔥 Fixing |

---

### 6. Closing & Preview (3 minutes)
- ⏰ 3:57-4:00 PM
- 🎯 **Goal:** Wrap up and preview next week

**Closing Items:**
- ✅ Recap top 3 action items
- 📅 Preview next week's focus
- 🙏 Thank participants
- 📝 Share recording link

**Next Session Preview:**
- Topic: [Theme based on this week's feedback]
- Prep: [Read update email before session]

---

## Facilitator Guide

### Before the Session
1. **Review Survey Results** (Day before)
   - Export Google Forms / Typeform data
   - Identify top trends in satisfaction, NPS, feature usage
   - Note significant changes week-over-week

2. **Review Slack Channel** (Day of)
   - Scan #task-tool-beta for bugs and feedback
   - Categorize by severity
   - Prepare bug triage spreadsheet

3. **Prepare Agenda Customization**
   - Adjust timing based on hot topics
   - Prepare discussion prompts for pain points
   - Pre-seed with 2-3 "wins" to start positive

### During the Session
- **Keep time rigorously** - Use timer visible to all
- **Encourage quiet voices** - Direct invite to unmute
- **Stay on topic** - Park "nice-to-haves" for later
- **Capture everything** - Assign a notetaker
- **End on time** - Respect participants' schedules

### After the Session
1. **Send Notes** (Within 2 hours)
   - Share action items with owners
   - Link to recording
   - Post in #task-tool-beta

2. **Update Tracking**
   - Log bugs in issue tracker (GitHub/Paperclip)
   - Create feature requests in backlog
   - Assign action items to team

3. **Measure Impact**
   - Track NPS trends weekly
   - Monitor bug resolution time
   - Feature adoption rates

---

## Participant Preparation Guide

**Before Joining:**
- ✅ Complete weekly survey (15 min)
- ✅ Review your week's usage
- ✅ Note specific pain points
- ✅ Come with 1 feature request

**During Session:**
- 💬 Speak up and be specific
- 🤝 Build on others' feedback
- 🎯 Focus on problems, not solutions
- ⏰ Be mindful of time

**After Session:**
- 📧 Check email for summary
- 🔗 Review action items
- 🐛 Keep reporting bugs in real-time

---

## First Session Script (Week 3)

**Opening:**
> "Welcome to our first weekly beta feedback session! This is YOUR time to shape the product. We'll spend the next hour reviewing what's working, what's not, and what you need next. We've got your survey results and want to hear directly from you. Let's start with wins - what went well this week?"

**If Silence:**
> "Let me start - I noticed [X] feature is getting great use in the data. Who tried it? What did you think?"

**Closing:**
> "Thanks for your time and honesty. We've got 3 clear action items with owners. You'll see notes and the recording in #task-tool-beta within the hour. Keep the feedback coming - every bug report and suggestion makes the product better. See you next week!"
