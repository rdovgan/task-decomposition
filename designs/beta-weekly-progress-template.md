## Week-Specific Themes

### Week 1: Launch & First Impressions
**Focus:** Onboarding success, initial bugs, first-use experience
**Key metrics:** Sign-up rate, first task created, survey completion

### Week 2: Finding Rhythm
**Focus:** Repeat usage, identifying patterns, common workflows
**Key metrics:** Active users, feature adoption, bug trends

### Week 3: Deep Dive
**Focus:** Feature-specific feedback, power user patterns, edge cases
**Key metrics:** Power user %, feature depth, satisfaction

### Week 4: Mid-Beta Recap
**Focus:** Summarize learning, announce changes, set priorities for rest of beta
**Key metrics:** Overall satisfaction, NPS trend, top issues resolved

### Final Week: Wrap & Next Steps
**Focus:** Summary of beta, key learnings, transition plan
**Key metrics:** Final NPS, feature validation, readiness assessment

---

## Tone & Style Guidelines

**Do:**
✅ Be transparent about bugs and issues
✅ Celebrate wins (even small ones)
✅ Use emojis to add personality
✅ Keep it concise (skimmable in 2 minutes)
✅ Include specific data and quotes
✅ Show progress week-over-week
✅ Be human and conversational

**Don't:**
❌ Hide bad news
❌ Overpromise on timelines
❌ Use jargon or corporate speak
❌ Make it too long
❌ Ignore feedback
❌ Be overly formal

---

## Pre-Send Checklist

Before sending the weekly update:

- [ ] Data is accurate and up-to-date
- [ ] All links work (survey, feedback session, Slack)
- [ ] Quotes are attributed correctly (with permission)
- [ ] Tone is balanced (wins + challenges)
- [ ] Timelines are realistic
- [ ] Spelling and grammar checked
- [ ] Slack post drafted and ready
- [ ] PM has reviewed and approved
- [ ] Sensitive feedback is anonymized
- [ ] Executive summary (for CEO) is ready
