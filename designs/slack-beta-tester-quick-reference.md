# Quick Reference Guide - #task-tool-beta

**For Beta Testers**

## Channel Overview
`#task-tool-beta` is your home for communication, support, and collaboration during the Task Decomposition Tool beta program.

## Quick Links

📋 **[Bug Report Form]**  
📝 **[Weekly Feedback Survey]**  
📖 **[Beta Documentation]**  
🗓️ **Feedback Sessions:** Fridays 2-3pm PST

## Daily Usage

### When to Post
✅ **DO post:**
- Blockers preventing you from testing
- Questions about features or functionality
- Bug reports (use template!)
- Feature suggestions and ideas
- Win celebrations and positive feedback
- Collaboration requests with other testers

❌ **DON'T post:**
- Personal conversations (use DMs)
- Unrelated topics
- Sensitive information
- Large files (use shared drive)

## Quick Response Guide

| Situation | What to Do | Who to Tag |
|-----------|------------|------------|
| Critical bug can't continue testing | Post immediately with 🚨 | @Tech Lead |
| Feature question | Ask in channel | (wait for response) |
| UI/UX feedback | Post with screenshots | @Design Lead |
| General question | Ask in channel | (wait for response) |
| Want to share success | Post with 🎉 | (everyone) |

## Bug Reporting

**Use this template:**
```
🐛 Bug Report
Title: [Brief description]
Severity: Critical/High/Medium/Low
Steps: 1. 2. 3.
Expected: [What should happen]
Actual: [What actually happened]
```

## Response Times

- 🚨 **Critical:** 2 hours
- ❓ **Questions:** 4-8 hours  
- 🐛 **Bugs:** 24 hours
- 📋 **Non-urgent:** 48 hours

## Pro Tips

💡 **Be Specific:** The more details you provide, the faster we can help

💡 **Use Threads:** Keep conversations organized by threading responses

💡 **Search First:** Your question might already be answered

💡 **Celebrate Wins:** Found something you love? Share it with 🎉

💡 **Be Constructive:** Frame feedback as "this could be better if..."

## Weekly Rhythm

**Every Week:**
- Monday: Weekly feedback survey opens
- Friday: Feedback session (2-3pm PST)
- Ongoing: Bug reports and questions

## Need Help?

**Quick Help:**
- Tag @PM for program questions
- Tag @Tech Lead for technical issues
- Check pinned posts for templates and links

**Stuck?**
1. Search the channel first
2. Check the beta documentation
3. Post your question in the channel
4. Join Friday feedback sessions

## Channel Etiquette

✅ **Do:**
- Be respectful and constructive
- Use threads for long conversations
- Share both positive and negative feedback
- Help other testers when you can
- Keep responses timely and focused

❌ **Don't:**
- Post sensitive company information
- Spam the channel with repeated messages
- Engage in heated debates
- Ignore channel guidelines
- Post large files directly (use links)

## Emojis We Use

🚨 = Critical blocker  
🐛 = Bug report  
❓ = Question  
💡 = Suggestion/idea  
🎉 = Win/celebration  
✅ = Completed task  
⏰ = Time-sensitive
